Indent Management

Turn internal purchase requests into approved, compared and ordered purchases, all inside Odoo.

How it works

Employees raise an indent. The right approver reviews it. Purchase creates RFQs, compares vendor quotes side by side, and confirms the best one as a purchase order.

  • Draft: the requester lists the products and quantities.
  • Waiting Approval: the department approver approves or rejects with a reason.
  • Approved: purchase raises RFQs to one or more vendors.
  • RFQ Created: compare the quotes and select one.
  • Partially Issued / Done: track receipts until the indent is closed.

Features

  • Indent requests with priority (Low, Normal, High, Urgent) and chatter tracking
  • Department-based approval. Requesters cannot approve their own indent.
  • Rejection wizard that records the reason
  • Create RFQs from an indent and compare them in one view
  • Printable RFQ report and RFQ email template
  • Final destination location and analytic project on each indent
  • Product Demand and Demand by Destination reports
  • Three access levels: User, Approver and Manager

Requirements

Odoo 19.0 with Purchase, Inventory, Employees and Accounting installed. Requesters need an employee record linked to their user so the department can be found.
